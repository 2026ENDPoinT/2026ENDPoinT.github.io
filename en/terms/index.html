Article 1

Purpose

These Terms set out the conditions and procedures for using the website operated by the ENDPoinT Organizing Committee (the “Committee”) and for applying to and taking part in the ENDPoinT hackathon, and the rights and obligations of the Committee and Participants.

Article 2

Definitions

  1. “Site” means https://2026endpoint.github.io and its subpages, which are operated by the Committee.
  2. “Event” means ENDPoinT, the union hackathon held jointly by the Seoul National University of Science and Technology (SeoulTech) academic clubs EC, NL, Darakbang, Plum and TCP, together with its preliminary programs (Session 1 and Session 2).
  3. “Committee” means the organizers of the above clubs’ union who plan and run the Event.
  4. “Participant” means a person who agrees to these Terms and applies to take part through the Site.
  5. “Work” means everything a Participant creates during the Event, including source code, services, designs and presentation materials.
Article 3

Posting and Changing These Terms

  1. The Committee posts these Terms on the Site.
  2. The Committee may change these Terms to the extent that it does not violate applicable laws and regulations. When it does, it will announce the changes and their effective date on the Site starting 7 days before they take effect.
  3. A Participant who does not agree to the changed Terms may cancel their participation.
Article 4

Applying

  1. To apply, you log in with a Google account and submit the application form on the Site. Submitting the application form is regarded as agreeing to these Terms.
  2. Participants must not provide false information, and must not apply using another person’s Google account or information.
  3. Each person may submit only one application form. If you submit again with the same Google account, your existing application form is replaced with the new content.
  4. The recruitment period is announced on the Site, and applications submitted after the period ends may not be accepted.
Article 5

Eligibility and Selection

  1. The Event recruits students of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at SeoulTech. You may apply whether or not you belong to a club, but students on leave of absence cannot participate.
  2. The maximum number of participants is 120. If the number of applicants exceeds the maximum, the Committee will select participants according to criteria it sets, and will announce the criteria and the results.
Article 6

Forming Teams

  1. A team consists of at least 2 and at most 6 members.
  2. For Participants who apply without a team, the Committee matches them with a team based on the preferred role, tech stack and other details in their application form.
  3. To balance the number of members across teams, the Committee may adjust team composition in consultation with Participants. The final teams will be announced at Session 1 (Nov 4).
Article 7

Event Schedule and Operation

  1. The Event schedule is as follows, and details are provided on the Site and in announcements.
    • Session 1 — Nov 4 (Wed), 2026: team announcement and training
    • Session 2 — Nov 11 (Wed), 2026: speaker sessions and mentoring
    • Main hackathon — Nov 13 (Fri) – 14 (Sat), 2026, Room 109, Mirae Building
  2. All Participants must attend the main hackathon (Nov 13 – 14). If you find it difficult to attend Session 1 or Session 2, please indicate this in advance on your application form.
  3. The Committee may change the schedule or venue for unavoidable reasons such as the academic calendar, facility circumstances or weather, and will announce any such change without delay.
Article 8

Participant Obligations

Participants must not do any of the following.

  1. Threatening or upsetting other Participants, organizers or speakers, including through verbal abuse, harassment, discrimination or sexual harassment.
  2. Using another person’s work or code without permission, or using it without complying with its license. Open source may be used if you comply with its license and credit the source.
  3. Submitting an existing capstone design project, or a project that is substantially the same, as a Work (duplicate submission). Capstone design projects are exhibited and awarded separately, so this rule is meant to prevent the same Work from winning an award twice.
  4. Cheating, including violating the Event rules set by the Committee or interfering with judging.
  5. Damaging school facilities or equipment, or violating the school’s facility use regulations.
  6. Any other act that violates applicable laws and regulations or these Terms.
Article 9

Rights to Your Work

  1. Intellectual property rights in a Work, including copyright, belong to the Participant (team) who created it.
  2. For Event records and promotion, the Committee may post the name, description, presentation materials and demo screens of a Work, free of charge, on the Site and on club and department channels. This does not transfer any rights in the Work to the Committee.
  3. A Participant who does not want a post under paragraph 2 may ask the Committee, and the Committee will not post it or will remove it.
Article 10

Filming and Promotion

  1. The Committee may take photos and videos during the Event for records and promotion.
  2. If a Participant who does not want to be filmed or posted tells the on-site organizers or the contact address, the Committee will exclude them from filming or blur their face before posting.
Article 11

Judging and Awards

  1. Judging combines the scores of a panel of judges (75%) and a participant vote (25%). The panel is made up of professors, alumni and industry professionals, and club presidents. Detailed criteria for each track will be announced before the Event.
  2. The total prize money is 3 million KRW, covering the Hackathon track and the Implementation track together. Prize money and prizes for each award category will be announced on the Site as soon as they are confirmed.
  3. If cheating is confirmed, the Committee may revoke the award and demand the return of any prize money or prizes already given.
  4. If you object to the judging results, you may contact the Committee, which will review the matter and reply.
Article 12

Restriction and Cancellation of Participation

  1. If a Participant has put false information on the application form or has violated Article 8, the Committee may issue a warning, restrict participation or disqualify them. Unless the violation is serious, the Participant is first given an opportunity to explain.
  2. If a Work is judged to be an existing capstone design project or one that is substantially the same (Article 8, item 3), the Committee may disqualify the team concerned. If this is found after awards have been given, the Committee may revoke the award and demand the return of any prize money or prizes already given.
  3. A Participant may cancel their participation at any time by notifying the contact address.
Article 13

Using the Site

  1. The Site is provided free of charge.
  2. The Site runs on GitHub Pages and Google services, so it may be temporarily unavailable during maintenance or outages of those services.
  3. If content posted on the Team board goes against Article 8, the Committee may edit or delete it.
Article 14

Limitation of Liability

  1. The Committee is not liable for damages caused by reasons beyond its control, such as natural disasters or outages of external services.
  2. The Committee is not liable for the loss or damage of personal belongings that Participants bring, or for disputes between Participants, unless the Committee acted intentionally or with gross negligence.
Article 15

Protection of Personal Information

The Committee handles Participants’ personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act and the Privacy Policy posted on the Site.

Article 16

Dispute Resolution

  1. Disputes relating to these Terms or the Event will be resolved by the Committee and the Participant through good-faith discussion.
  2. These Terms are interpreted in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Korea.
Addendum

These Terms take effect on Oct 7, 2026.

01

Purposes of processing personal information

The Organizing Committee processes only the minimum personal information necessary for the following purposes.

  • Receiving hackathon applications, verifying the applicant's identity, and preventing duplicate applications
  • Checking eligibility to participate, matching teams, and supporting team formation
  • Delivering announcements, and event-related and emergency contact
  • Running the judging and awards, and confirming winners
  • Event operations such as providing souvenirs, meals, and prizes

We do not use personal information for any other purpose. If the purpose changes, we will notify you in advance in accordance with applicable laws and, where required, obtain your consent again.

03

Retention and destruction

Retention period

  • We keep personal information from the time you apply until it is destroyed within 3 months after the event has ended and the awards and settlement are complete.
  • To document awards and handle objections, only winners' information is kept for up to 1 year after the event ends, and then destroyed.
  • If you ask to cancel your participation, the relevant application information is destroyed without delay.
  • Photos and videos posted for promotion are kept for as long as the post remains, and are deleted without delay if you ask for them to be taken down.

Destruction method

  • The relevant sheets and rows in Google Sheets are permanently deleted so that they cannot be recovered (including emptying the trash).
  • Copies downloaded by the organizers, such as Excel files, are deleted at the same time.
  • Materials printed on paper are shredded or incinerated.
04

Third-party provision and disclosure

The Organizing Committee processes personal information only within the purposes in Section 01, and does not provide it to outside parties except in the following cases.

  • When the data subject has consented in advance. If information must be passed to the host institution or sponsors for event insurance or for awarding certificates and prize money, we will first tell you the recipient, the items, and the purpose, and obtain your consent.
  • When there is a special provision in law, or when an investigative agency or similar body requests it following the procedures set by law

Team board

Only if you request to recruit teammates or to find a team, we post your club, preferred role, and a summary of your tech stack on the Team board. We do not post your name, contact number, email, or student ID.

Applying to a team

When a user applies to a team on the Team board, the club, tech stack and development experience, whether you can attend the full schedule, and message written in the team application are visible to that team's leader (the person who posted). Your name and contact number are disclosed only in the following cases.

  • Name — when the applicant chose “Show my name to the team leader even before acceptance” in the team application, or after the team leader accepts the application
  • Contact number (mobile phone number) — only after the team leader accepts the application. At that time, the team leader's name and contact number are disclosed to the applicant in the same way.

This disclosure is something the user chooses directly through the application and acceptance process, and is used only for contact between team members and for confirming team composition. Email addresses and unique Google account identifiers are not disclosed to other users. When an application is accepted, the applicant's other pending applications to other teams are automatically closed.

When a new application arrives and when an application is accepted, a notification email is sent to the Google account email address of the user concerned. The email contains no personal information such as name or contact number, and includes only the site address.

06

Handling of Google account data

This site uses Google login to verify applicants. The information received from your Google account and how it is used are as follows.

  • Scopes requested — We request only the default Google login scopes (openid, email, profile).
  • Use — Used only to verify the applicant's identity, prevent duplicate applications, and update the existing application form when you submit again with the same account.
  • Storage — Only your email address and unique account identifier are stored together with the application form. The login token is used only to check its authenticity with Google's servers at submission, and is not stored.
  • No sharing — We do not sell Google account information, use it for advertising, or pass it to third parties. We do not access other Google data such as Gmail or Drive. (Team application notification emails are sent from an organizer account, and we do not access the user's Gmail.)

The way the Organizing Committee uses information received through Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect this site at any time from third-party app management in your Google Account.

07

Installation, operation, and refusal of automatic collection tools

  • The Organizing Committee does not use cookies or visitor analytics tools.
  • An application form in progress is temporarily saved in the local storage of the user's browser so that it is not lost when the page is refreshed. This content stays only on the user's device and is not sent to the Organizing Committee, and it is deleted once you finish submitting. It is also deleted if you clear site data in your browser settings.
  • During Google login, Google may use its own cookies, which are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
08

Security measures

  • The spreadsheet containing application information can be viewed only by those organizers who need it for their work, and sharing as "anyone with the link" is not used.
  • Permission to edit and deploy the site (the GitHub repository and the Google Apps Script that receives application forms) is also limited to the organizers.
  • Application forms are sent over HTTPS, and the Google login token is verified with Google's servers at every submission to prevent submissions in someone else's name.
  • Copies downloaded as Excel files or similar are kept only on devices used for operations, and are deleted together with the originals when the retention period ends.
09

Rights of data subjects and how to exercise them

  • Participants may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of the processing of their personal information.
  • If you make a request to the contact email in Section 10, we will act on it without delay, and within 10 days at the latest, and let you know the result.
  • To change your application, sign in again with the same Google account and resubmit the application form. The existing application form will be replaced with the new content.
  • If you ask to cancel your participation, your application information is destroyed without delay. If you delete required information or ask for processing to be suspended, your participation may be cancelled.
  • You may also make a request through a legal representative or an authorized agent.
